# 🪝 The Ultimate Guide to Content Hooks

## What Makes a Hook Work?
A great hook does 3 things:
1. **Stops the scroll** — pattern interrupt
2. **Creates curiosity** — open loop
3. **Promises value** — worth their time

## The 10 Hook Frameworks

### 1. The Contradiction Hook
"Everyone says [X]. They're dead wrong."

### 2. The Number Hook
"[X] things that [outcome] (most people only know 2)"

### 3. The Story Hook
"[X years] ago I was [bad situation]. Today [amazing result]."

### 4. The Question Hook
"What if [desirable outcome] was actually [surprisingly simple]?"

### 5. The Authority Hook
"After [X years] and [impressive number], here's what I know:"

### 6. The Warning Hook
"Stop [common action]. It's killing your [important thing]."

### 7. The Secret Hook
"The [topic] strategy nobody talks about:"

### 8. The Results Hook
"My client [specific result] in [short timeframe]. Here's the exact system:"

### 9. The Controversial Hook
"Unpopular opinion: [bold contrarian take]"

### 10. The Curiosity Gap Hook
"I discovered [something unexpected]. It changed everything about [topic]."
